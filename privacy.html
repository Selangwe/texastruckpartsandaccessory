Who we are

Texas Truck Parts & Accessory is a family-run truck parts yard in Sugar Land, Texas, selling OEM factory take-offs and heavy-duty aftermarket steel. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean that business, and "the site" means this website.

  • 13618 Florence Rd, Ste D1, Sugar Land, TX 77498
  • Parts desk: (952) 529-3586, Monday to Saturday
  • Email: Support.ranchhand@gmail.com

What we collect

Information you give us

You can browse the entire catalogue without telling us anything. We only receive personal information when you choose to send it — by submitting the part request form, starting an order, messaging us on WhatsApp, emailing, or calling. Depending on which of those you use, that can include:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • A shipping address, or a note that you are collecting from the yard
  • Your vehicle's year, make and model, and sometimes a VIN if you ask us to confirm fitment against it
  • The parts you are asking about, and anything else you write in the message

Information collected automatically

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel records ordinary request logs — your IP address, the page requested, the time, and your browser's user-agent string — which exist to serve the page and to keep the service secure and available.

Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts. Requesting a font file discloses your IP address to Google, which is unavoidable while the fonts are served from Google's network. Nothing else on the site loads from a third party.

What stays on your device

Some things that look like they are "stored" are stored in your own browser and are never transmitted to us unless you send an order:

  • Your order. The parts you add are kept in your browser's local storage so the order survives a reload or a closed tab. It stays on that device, in that browser.
  • Your contact details, if you have entered them. The order form remembers what you typed so you do not have to type it again.
  • Your saved vehicle. The year, make and model you pick in the fitment finder are kept in session storage, which your browser discards when you close the tab.

Clearing your browser's site data removes all of it. We cannot read any of it remotely, and we do not receive it until you actually submit an order or a message.

What we do not do

We do not run analytics on this site. There is no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no advertising pixel, no Meta pixel, no session recorder and no heatmap tool.

We do not set tracking cookies, and we do not use cookies to profile you or follow you across other websites.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with data brokers or advertising networks.

There are no user accounts on this site, so we never ask you to create a password and we do not hold one.

How we use it

We use what you send us to do the thing you contacted us about, and not for other purposes:

  • To answer your question, quote a part, or confirm whether something fits your truck
  • To take, confirm, pack and ship your order, or arrange collection from the yard
  • To take payment through the method you chose, and to deal with a refund or a problem
  • To keep our own records of sales, as any business must

We do not send marketing email. If that ever changes, it will be something you opt into, and every message will carry a way out.

Who else sees it

We share personal information only where it is needed to complete what you asked for:

  • Vercel, which hosts the site and handles the request logs described above.
  • Google, which serves the typefaces and therefore sees your IP address.
  • WhatsApp and Meta, if you choose to send your order or message over WhatsApp. That conversation lives in Meta's service and is governed by their privacy policy as well as this one. You can email or call instead if you would rather it did not.
  • Google (Gmail), which carries our email, if you contact us that way.
  • Shipping carriers, which need the delivery address and a contact number.
  • Payment and financing providers, as described below.
  • Anyone we are legally required to share it with, such as in response to a lawful request, or where it is necessary to establish or defend a legal claim.

Payments and financing

This site does not take payment. There is no card form anywhere on it, and no card details are ever typed into, transmitted by, or stored on this website.

When you pay by Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, bank transfer or cryptocurrency, that transaction happens inside your own bank's or provider's app, on their systems and under their privacy policy. What we see is that a payment arrived and who it came from.

Financing through Snap Finance, Acima or Progressive Leasing is an application made to that company, not to us. They collect their own information, run their own checks, and are responsible for it under their own privacy policies. Approval is theirs to give, not ours.

How long we keep it

We keep order and enquiry records for as long as we need them to support the sale — for warranty questions, returns, disputes and our own accounting and tax obligations — and then no longer than is reasonable. Messages that do not lead anywhere are cleared out in the ordinary course. If you want your information deleted sooner, ask, and see the next section.

Your rights in Texas

The Texas Data Privacy and Security Act gives Texas residents rights over personal information held about them. Subject to the exceptions in the Act, you may ask us to:

  • Confirm whether we hold personal information about you, and give you a copy
  • Correct something we hold that is wrong
  • Delete what we hold about you
  • Stop selling your personal data or using it for targeted advertising — which we do not do in the first place, and have no plans to start

To exercise any of these, email Support.ranchhand@gmail.com or call (952) 529-3586. We will need enough information to be sure it is really you before we act, which for an order is usually the name, phone number and rough date of the order. We will respond within the time the law allows. If we refuse, we will tell you why, and you may appeal by replying to that decision; if an appeal is refused you may complain to the Texas Attorney General.

If you are outside Texas, write to us anyway. We will do the same thing.

Children

This is a truck parts yard. The site is meant for adults who can enter into a contract, it is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date at the top. The date currently at the top is the date the version you are reading took effect. Material changes to how we handle information will not be applied retroactively to information already collected without telling you.
