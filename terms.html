What we sell

Most of the catalogue is OEM factory take-offs: genuine manufacturer parts removed from new or nearly new trucks, usually because the original owner swapped them for something else before the vehicle was driven. They are original equipment, not copies, and they are not new-in-box. Expect the condition described on the listing.

We also sell new aftermarket steel, such as Ranch Hand bumpers and grille guards. Those are new goods and are described as such.

Every part is a specific physical item in the yard. Photographs are of the actual item wherever we have been able to shoot it; where a listing uses a manufacturer or representative image, the condition notes on that listing govern, not the photograph. Colours shown on a screen are not an exact match for paint in daylight.

Fitment

The year, make and model data on this site is a guide to narrow the search. Trims, cab configurations, bed lengths, sensor and camera options and mid-year production changes all affect whether a part actually bolts to your truck.

Confirm fitment with us before you buy. Call the parts desk with your VIN and we will check the part against your specific vehicle. That check is free and it is the single best way to avoid a return.

If you order without asking us to confirm, and the part does not fit, that is treated as a change-of-mind return rather than our error.

Prices

Prices are in US dollars. Advertised prices for delivery to the lower 48 states include freight, so the price you are quoted is the price you pay — there is no shipping charge added later.

Prices, availability and discounts can change without notice, and a listing being visible is not a guarantee that the item is still on the floor. Every part here is a single physical item, so the same part can be sold to somebody standing in the yard while you are reading about it.

We try hard to keep the catalogue accurate, but if a part is listed at an obviously wrong price, or is listed after it has already sold, we may decline the order rather than fulfil it. If you have already paid in that situation, you get a full refund. That is the whole remedy.

How an order is made

This site does not have an automated checkout, and nothing you do on it takes payment. Adding parts and submitting your details sends the yard an order request — an offer to buy — which someone then reads.

A contract exists only once we confirm the order back to you and tell you it is available. Up to that point we may decline, or come back to you about availability, condition or fitment. This matters most where two people ask for the same one-of-a-kind part on the same day.

Payment

We take Zelle, Venmo, Apple Pay, bank transfer and cryptocurrency, and we can discuss other methods at the desk. Payment is arranged after we confirm the order.

No payment is taken on this website and no card details are entered anywhere on it. Each method runs on its own provider's platform under that provider's terms.

Cryptocurrency amounts are set against the price in US dollars at the time we agree them. Because networks and rates move, we confirm the network and the amount with you before you send anything; we are not responsible for coins sent to an address or on a network we did not confirm.

Parts are held for you once payment clears, not when it is sent.

Those are separate companies and separate agreements between you and them — we are not the lender, we do not make the decision, and approval is never guaranteed. Their terms, fees and payment schedules are theirs and you should read them. Any advertised monthly figure on this site is an illustration, not an offer of credit.

Shipping and pickup

Shipping is free to the lower 48 states on the parts advertised that way. Alaska, Hawaii and anywhere outside the United States are quoted separately, if we can ship there at all — bumpers, beds and racks are large freight items and some destinations are impractical.

Local pickup is available from the yard in Sugar Land. Bring your order details and photo ID.

Delivery dates we give are estimates. Freight carriers run to their own schedules and we do not control them, so we are not liable for a late delivery, though we will chase it with you.

Inspect freight before you sign for it. Large parts travel on pallets and can be damaged in transit. Check the item in front of the driver, and note any damage on the delivery paperwork before signing. A signed-clean delivery makes a damage claim very much harder to win, for us as well as for you. Photograph anything that looks wrong and send it to us the same day.

Risk in the goods passes to you on delivery, or on collection if you are picking up.

Returns

If something is wrong — the part is not what was described, it arrived damaged, or we shipped the wrong item — contact the parts desk and we will put it right.

Start every return by contacting us before shipping anything back, so we can agree what is happening and where it goes. Parts sent back without being agreed first can go astray in a working yard, and we cannot be responsible for those.

To be returned, a part needs to be in the condition it was sent in: not fitted, not drilled, not cut, not painted, not modified. Once a part has been mounted to a truck or refinished it cannot go back.

Where the fault is ours, we cover the return freight and refund you in full. Where the return is a change of mind or an unconfirmed fitment, the arrangements are agreed with you at the desk before the part moves.

Refunds go back by the method you paid with, once the part is back and inspected.

Condition and warranty

Take-off parts are used goods. They are sold in the condition described on their listing and in the condition notes, which is where any scratch, scuff or blemish worth mentioning is recorded. Ask us for more photographs before ordering if the condition matters to you — we would far rather send pictures than process a return.

New aftermarket goods carry whatever warranty their manufacturer provides, and that warranty is given by the manufacturer, not by us.
